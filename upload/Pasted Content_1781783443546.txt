3B.8 Phase 7 – Distressed Cargo Resolution (Optional)
Goal: When cargo becomes distressed (physically damaged, abandoned, delayed beyond shelf life, or subject to demurrage), the platform provides a structured resolution workflow. The distressed cargo owner (typically the seller, but could be the buyer or logistics provider) can declare distress, receive AIassessed condition and pricing, and resolve the lot via sale, compliant disposal, or insurance claim. A microcontract is created for the distressed portion, with a separate SGTX fee (1.5% adjusted by countryspecific distressed factor). The original trade SGTX fee remains collected (already paid). The “Check Buyers” advisory feature allows the seller to see potential buyers from their saved contacts before initiating outreach. All actions are oneclick.
Prerequisite: A shipment (or a specific shipment within a multishipment contract) has status IN_EXECUTION or DELIVERED but with damaged goods. The original contract is locked and the SGTX trade fee has already been paid by the seller’s side. The cargo owner has the necessary permission (distressed.declare). For multishipment contracts, distress can be declared per shipment without affecting other shipments.
Oneclick guarantee at end of Phase 7: Seller clicks “Declare Distressed” (one click) → AI condition assessment and pricing run automatically. Seller clicks “Start Outreach” (one click) after selecting buyers. Buyer clicks “Accept Offer” (one click) → microcontract created and locked automatically after distressed fee payment.
AI Integration in Phase 7:
• 🧠 A1 (Groq → Ollama): Triage dashboard explanations, price recommendation summaries, outreach message generation, privacy notice.
• 🧠 A2 (HF local → Ollama): Condition assessment (HF ViT), dynamic pricing (XGBoost), “Check Buyers” ranking (LightGBM).
• 🧠 A3 (HF + Groq → Ollama): Escalation for unusual price deviations, manual compliance review for certain jurisdictions.
• 🧠 A4 (OPA + WasmEdge): Microcontract lock, distressed fee calculation (1.5% × country factor), privacy notice optin enforcement.
Critical Addons used in Phase 7:
• ⚡ GNN Risk Engine – not directly used, but may flag distressed buyers with sanctions links.
• ⚡ Causal Inference – root cause analysis for distress (e.g., “68% due to temperature excursion”) included in evidence package.
• ⚡ ZK Proofs – optional for confidential distressed sale terms.

3B.8.10 Microcontract & Separate SGTX Fee – One Click to Lock

• microUSTN
• Buyer and seller (distressed cargo buyer)
• Agreed price
• Special distressed terms

• Original SGTX trade fee already paid for full shipment.
• For the distressed portion, a new, separate SGTX fee is charged on the distressed sale amount.

3B.9 Phase 8 – Dispute Resolution & Arbitration
Goal: If a party to a trade believes that the executed shipment, payment, financing, or distressed sale has not met the contractual terms – quality defects, late delivery, documentation errors, temperature deviations, payment default, or fee miscalculation – they can file a dispute under the USTN (or financing agreement ID, or microUSTN). The platform provides a structured, AIassisted mediation process, with the option to escalate to international arbitration (ICC, DIFCLCIA). The affected FeeLock(s) are frozen (administratively). The system ensures that all evidence is cryptographically verifiable, offers AIpowered outcome prediction, settlement proposal generation, document authenticity checks, and thirdparty expert invitation. For multishipment contracts, disputes can be filed per shipment. For financing disputes (including cofinancing), the evidence package includes the financing agreement and repayment records. The QC dispute fasttrack automatically flags inspectoroverridden AI findings. All actions are oneclick or voiceinitiated.
Prerequisite: The trade (or specific shipment, or financing agreement, or distressed microcontract) is locked or settled. The disputing party has the permission dispute.file.
Oneclick guarantee at end of Phase 8: Party clicks “File Dispute” (one click) → evidence autocompiled. Both parties participate in mediation with oneclick messages, offers, and acceptances. If settlement reached, one click to accept proposal → settlement addendum signed automatically. If arbitration needed, one click to prepare case file → downloadable PDF ready.
AI Integration in Phase 8:
• 🧠 A1 (Groq → Ollama): Mediation log translation, settlement proposal generation, plainlanguage summaries, voiceinitiated filing.
• 🧠 A2 (HF local → Ollama): Dispute triage categorisation, evidence autocompilation (including QC override flagging), predictive outcome modelling (XGBoost), document authenticity checks.
• 🧠 A3 (HF + Groq → Ollama): Escalation to human review, thirdparty expert invitation workflow, negotiation deadlock resolution, arbitration case preparation.
• 🧠 A4 (OPA + WasmEdge): FeeLock freeze on dispute filing, state transitions, settlement addendum signature enforcement.
Critical Addons used in Phase 8:
• ⚡ Causal Inference Engine (Addon 3) – root cause analysis for delay/quality failures, automatically included in evidence.
• ⚡ GNN Risk Engine – not directly used, but may flag sanctions links in evidence.
• ⚡ ZK Proofs – optional for private settlement proofs.

3B.9.10 Document Authenticity Check – Automatic (🧠 A2)

3B.9.11 QC Dispute FastTrack – Automatic Flagging

Oneclick: No action – automatically included. Mediator sees the overrides highlighted.

3B.9.12 Escalation to International Arbitration – One Click to Prepare Case
If mediation fails (after max rounds – default 5 – or either party clicks “Escalate to Arbitration”), the system prepares the case for formal arbitration.
Automated Case Filing (🧠 A2, A1):
1. Retrieves the arbitration request form from a local template (preobtained from ICC, DIFCLCIA, CRCICA, etc.).
2. Autofills with dispute details, parties, USTN, contract references, and a structured claim statement.
3. Groq drafts the claim narrative in the language required by the arbitration body.
4. The compiled package (form + evidence package + Loom hash) is produced as a PDF, stored in generated_documents.
5. The filing party’s legal counsel reviews and submits (outside the platform).
Oneclick: Any party clicks “Prepare Arbitration Case” – one click. System generates the PDF. Download – one click. SGTX does not submit the case; only prepares documents.
Smart Inbox: Both parties receive an informational item: “Arbitration case package ready. Download PDF.” Dispute status → ARBITRATION_PENDING.
Governance (G10U8): The platform does not submit the case; only prepares documents.
 
3B.9.13 SGTX Fee Dispute (Separate Workflow) – One Click to Initiate
Tenant action: In Trade Command Center’s financial card, click “Dispute SGTX Fee” – one click.
Form prefilled with USTN, fee amount, rate, and a reason (mandatory). Submit – one click.
Processing (🧠 A2, HF local):
• AI compares the fee calculation against the contract’s parameters and the dynamic formula.
• Checks if any adjustment factor was misapplied (e.g., wrong perishability surcharge, incorrect country pair).
• Outputs recommendation: UPHOLD, ADJUST (partial refund), or REFUND.
Escalation: Dispute is escalated to the Platform Governance Authority (multisig). A new Governor decision with decision_type = 'SGTX_FEE_DISPUTE' is created.
Authority reviews the AI analysis and can:
• Uphold the fee (no change)
• Approve a refund (partial or full)
• Request more information
If refund approved: payment-orchestrator initiates a refund to the original PSP method (or bank transfer). FeeLock adjusted (marked PARTIALLY_RELEASED).
Oneclick per step: Tenant clicks “Dispute” → submit (1). Authority reviews and clicks “Approve Refund” (1) → refund processed automatically.
Time limit: Must be filed within 90 days of fee payment.
