   486→   486→
   487→   487→14.0 Purpose & Design Philosophy
   488→   488→
   489→   489→The SGTX platform handles sensitive trade data, financial transactions,
   490→   490→and cryptographic identities across multiple jurisdictions. A robust
   491→   491→security model is not optional — it is a constitutional requirement.
   492→   492→This part defines the comprehensive threat model for the SGTX platform
   493→   493→using industry‑standard frameworks:
   494→   494→
   495→   495→-   STRIDE (Spoofing, Tampering, Repudiation, Information Disclosure,
   496→   496→    Denial of Service, Elevation of Privilege) — per component, per
   497→   497→    trust boundary.
   498→   498→
   499→   499→-   MITRE ATT&CK — adversary tactics, techniques, and procedures (TTPs)
   500→   500→    relevant to trade platforms.
   501→   501→
   502→   502→All mitigations are zero‑cost, built on open‑source tools (OPA,
   503→   503→WasmEdge, Cilium, Falco, etc.) and free threat intelligence feeds. AI
   504→   504→(Groq, Hugging Face) is used for advisory purposes — e.g., summarising
   505→   505→threat findings, generating post‑mortems, and suggesting remediation
