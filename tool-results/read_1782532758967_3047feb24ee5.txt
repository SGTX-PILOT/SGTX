     1→     1→9.0 Purpose & Design Philosophy
     2→     2→
     3→     3→Logistics providers are the backbone of physical trade execution. SGTX
     4→     4→treats each provider type as a first‑class participant with its own
     5→     5→portal, service catalogue, quotation workflow, and automated invoicing.
     6→     6→The platform does not mandate any provider; rather, it enables sellers
     7→     7→and buyers to discover, quote, select, and pay providers that they
     8→     8→already know (via the Network feature) within the trade workflow. The
     9→     9→platform never suggests providers, never ranks them, and never offers
    10→    10→"you might also like" — all relationships are explicit and pre‑existing.

   158→   158→9.1.2 Quotation & Acceptance Workflow (Trucking)
   159→   159→
   160→   160→Step 1 — Seller requests quote (during Phase 2 — Logistics Builder).
   161→   161→Seller selects "Trucking" service, enters pickup and delivery addresses,
   162→   162→container type, weight. System calculates estimated distance (OSRM) and
   163→   163→displays a dropdown of eligible LSPs that the seller has previously
   164→   164→worked with or added to contacts (never a list of "top rated"
   165→   165→strangers). Seller selects "Fast Trucking Co."
   166→   166→
   167→   167→Step 2 — LSP receives request (Smart Inbox): "New trucking request for
   168→   168→USTN ... — pick up 22 pallets frozen strawberries from Beheira to
   169→   169→Damietta. Estimated distance 150 km. Send quote."
   170→   170→
   171→   171→Step 3 — LSP sends quote: LSP clicks "Send Quote". Pre‑filled from
   172→   172→catalogue ($0.85/km × 150 km = $127.50). LSP can adjust.
   173→   173→
   174→   174→json
   175→   175→
   176→   176→POST /v1/lsp/quote
   177→   177→
   178→   178→{
   179→   179→
   180→   180→"ustn": "SGTX-...",
   181→   181→
   182→   182→"service_type": "TRUCKING",
   183→   183→
   184→   184→"fee": 127.50,
   185→   185→
   186→   186→"currency": "USD",
   187→   187→
   188→   188→"valid_until": "2026-04-17T23:59:59Z",
   189→   189→
   190→   190→"notes": "Includes fuel, driver, insurance up to $50k"
   191→   191→
   192→   192→}
   193→   193→
   194→   194→Step 4 — Seller accepts quote: Seller sees quote in Smart Inbox, clicks
   195→   195→"Accept". SGTX generates invoice for $127.50 (ETA‑compliant), adds to
   196→   196→Stage 1 payment plan.
   197→   197→
   198→   198→Step 5 — Payment collection: Seller pays Stage 1 (which includes this
   199→   199→fee). PSP split sends $127.50 to LSP (minus platform fee). LSP receives
   200→   200→Smart Inbox: "Payment received. You may now dispatch the truck."
   201→   201→
   202→   202→Step 6 — Service delivery: Driver uses mobile app (offline) to scan
   203→   203→pallet barcodes, confirm pickup, and delivery. GPS tracks route. After
   204→   204→delivery, LSP marks milestone "Delivered". Shipment status updates.
   205→   205→
   206→   206→Step 7 — Dispute (if any): Buyer claims goods damaged due to rough
   207→   207→handling. Evidence package includes GPS trace, speed logs, driver notes.
   208→   208→AI‑generated causal analysis attributes damage to route roughness.
   209→   209→Settlement proposed.
   210→   210→
   211→   211→AI Authority: A1 for quote price suggestions (Groq); A2 for route
   212→   212→optimisation (OSRM, rule‑based); A4 for milestone confirmation.
