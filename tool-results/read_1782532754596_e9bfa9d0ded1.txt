     1→9.0 Purpose & Design Philosophy
     2→
     3→Logistics providers are the backbone of physical trade execution. SGTX
     4→treats each provider type as a first‑class participant with its own
     5→portal, service catalogue, quotation workflow, and automated invoicing.
     6→The platform does not mandate any provider; rather, it enables sellers
     7→and buyers to discover, quote, select, and pay providers that they
     8→already know (via the Network feature) within the trade workflow. The
     9→platform never suggests providers, never ranks them, and never offers
    10→"you might also like" — all relationships are explicit and pre‑existing.

   158→9.1.2 Quotation & Acceptance Workflow (Trucking)
   159→
   160→Step 1 — Seller requests quote (during Phase 2 — Logistics Builder).
   161→Seller selects "Trucking" service, enters pickup and delivery addresses,
   162→container type, weight. System calculates estimated distance (OSRM) and
   163→displays a dropdown of eligible LSPs that the seller has previously
   164→worked with or added to contacts (never a list of "top rated"
   165→strangers). Seller selects "Fast Trucking Co."
   166→
   167→Step 2 — LSP receives request (Smart Inbox): "New trucking request for
   168→USTN ... — pick up 22 pallets frozen strawberries from Beheira to
   169→Damietta. Estimated distance 150 km. Send quote."
   170→
   171→Step 3 — LSP sends quote: LSP clicks "Send Quote". Pre‑filled from
   172→catalogue ($0.85/km × 150 km = $127.50). LSP can adjust.
   173→
   174→json
   175→
   176→POST /v1/lsp/quote
   177→
   178→{
   179→
   180→"ustn": "SGTX-...",
   181→
   182→"service_type": "TRUCKING",
   183→
   184→"fee": 127.50,
   185→
   186→"currency": "USD",
   187→
   188→"valid_until": "2026-04-17T23:59:59Z",
   189→
   190→"notes": "Includes fuel, driver, insurance up to $50k"
   191→
   192→}
   193→
   194→Step 4 — Seller accepts quote: Seller sees quote in Smart Inbox, clicks
   195→"Accept". SGTX generates invoice for $127.50 (ETA‑compliant), adds to
   196→Stage 1 payment plan.
   197→
   198→Step 5 — Payment collection: Seller pays Stage 1 (which includes this
   199→fee). PSP split sends $127.50 to LSP (minus platform fee). LSP receives
   200→Smart Inbox: "Payment received. You may now dispatch the truck."
   201→
   202→Step 6 — Service delivery: Driver uses mobile app (offline) to scan
   203→pallet barcodes, confirm pickup, and delivery. GPS tracks route. After
   204→delivery, LSP marks milestone "Delivered". Shipment status updates.
   205→
   206→Step 7 — Dispute (if any): Buyer claims goods damaged due to rough
   207→handling. Evidence package includes GPS trace, speed logs, driver notes.
   208→AI‑generated causal analysis attributes damage to route roughness.
   209→Settlement proposed.
   210→
   211→AI Authority: A1 for quote price suggestions (Groq); A2 for route
   212→optimisation (OSRM, rule‑based); A4 for milestone confirmation.

   326→9.2.2 Quotation & Booking Workflow
   327→
   328→Step 1 — Seller requests booking (during Phase 2 — Logistics Builder).
   329→Seller selects "Ocean freight", enters port of loading, discharge,
   330→container type, desired sailing window. System displays eligible
   331→shipping lines that the seller has a contract with or has previously
   332→used (from saved contacts). Seller selects "MSC Egypt".
   333→
   334→Step 2 — Shipping line receives request (Smart Inbox): "New booking
   335→request for USTN ... — 20,000 kg frozen strawberries, Damietta →
   336→Hamburg, 2 × 40ft reefers, requested sailing 20 Jun. Send quote."
   337→
   338→Step 3 — SHIP sends quotation: Clicks "Send Quote". Pre‑filled from
   339→contract rate ($4,200 per container = $8,400 total). Shipping line can
   340→adjust (e.g., peak season surcharge).
   341→
   342→json
   343→
   344→POST /v1/ship/quote
   345→
   346→{
   347→
   348→"ustn": "SGTX-...",
   349→
   350→"service_type": "OCEAN_FREIGHT",
   351→
   352→"fee": 8400.00,
   353→
   354→"currency": "USD",
   355→
   356→"valid_until": "2026-04-20T23:59:59Z",
   357→
   358→"vessel": "MSC FIONA",
   359→
   360→"voyage": "MSF123E",
   361→
   362→"etd": "2026-04-20T08:00:00Z",
   363→
   364→"eta": "2026-05-05T14:00:00Z"
   365→
   366→}
   367→
   368→Step 4 — Seller accepts quote: SGTX generates invoice for $8,400 (added
   369→to Stage 2 payment plan — credit terms may apply).
   370→
   371→Step 5 — Booking confirmation: Shipping line confirms booking via portal
   372→or API → returns booking reference MAEU876543210. SGTX
   373→updates shipments.status = BOOKED.
   374→
   375→Step 6 — eBL issuance: After vessel loading, shipping line issues eBL
   376→via their platform. SGTX receives webhook (or polls) and stores eBL
   377→in documents. The eBL includes the USTN.
   378→
   379→Step 7 — Container release: After seller pays Stage 1 (and any mandatory
   380→Stage 2), SGTX sends container release confirmation (email + API token).
   381→Shipping line acknowledges; container gated out.
   382→
   383→Step 8 — Milestone updates: Shipping line updates milestone "Departed"
   384→(via portal or API). AIS feed automatically updates position. Buyer
   385→tracks.
   386→
   387→AI Authority: A1 for match score and sailing schedule suggestions; A2
   388→for eBL extraction (HF Donut); A4 for booking confirmation validation.
