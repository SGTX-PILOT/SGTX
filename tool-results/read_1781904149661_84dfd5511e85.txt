   766→   766→14.0 Purpose & Design Philosophy
   767→   767→
   768→   768→The SGTX platform handles sensitive trade data, financial transactions,
   769→   769→and cryptographic identities across multiple jurisdictions. A robust
   770→   770→security model is not optional — it is a constitutional requirement.
   771→   771→This part defines the comprehensive threat model for the SGTX platform
   772→   772→using industry‑standard frameworks:
   773→   773→
   774→   774→-   STRIDE (Spoofing, Tampering, Repudiation, Information Disclosure,
   775→   775→    Denial of Service, Elevation of Privilege) — per component, per
   776→   776→    trust boundary.
   777→   777→
   778→   778→-   MITRE ATT&CK — adversary tactics, techniques, and procedures (TTPs)
   779→   779→    relevant to trade platforms.
   780→   780→
   781→   781→All mitigations are zero‑cost, built on open‑source tools (OPA,
   782→   782→WasmEdge, Cilium, Falco, etc.) and free threat intelligence feeds. AI
   783→   783→(Groq, Hugging Face) is used for advisory purposes — e.g., summarising
   784→   784→threat findings, generating post‑mortems, and suggesting remediation
   785→   785→steps. Never autonomous blocking (A5 forbidden).
   786→   786→

   801→   801→
   802→   802→Key principles (non‑marketplace, orchestration‑only):
   803→   803→
   804→   804→-   Zero‑trust architecture — no implicit trust; every request is
   805→   805→    authenticated, authorised, and encrypted.
   806→   806→
   807→   807→-   Defence in depth — multiple layers of control (network, application,
   808→   808→    data, constitutional).
   809→   809→
   810→   810→-   Immutable audit — every security‑relevant event is logged immutably
   811→   811→    via Loom.
   812→   812→

   814→   814→    pentests, daily vulnerability scans.

   817→   817→    and verifiable.
