     1→PART 12G — UNIVERSAL COMMAND CENTER
     2→
     3→12G.0 Purpose & Design Philosophy
     4→
     5→The Universal Command Center serves as the primary landing page for all
     6→authenticated users before entering role‑specific portals. It provides a
     7→unified dashboard for trade visibility, pending actions, compliance
     8→alerts, financial status, workflow shortcuts, and AI assistant access —
     9→all in one place.
    10→
    11→Access: All authenticated users regardless of tenant type or role.
    12→
    13→Dual‑mode toggle: ✅ visible (if DUAL, shows combined view of both
    14→modes)
    15→
    16→Landing page: Default after login (user can set preference to bypass to
    17→role‑specific portal)
    18→
    19→Core principles:
    20→
    21→-   One‑click core — every action from the Command Center executes with
    22→    a single click
    23→
    24→-   Role‑adaptive — content adapts to the user's role and permissions
    25→
    26→-   Real‑time — all cards and metrics update via NATS WebSocket
    27→
    28→-   AI‑assisted — AI Operations Assistant provides context‑aware
    29→    guidance
    30→
    31→-   Non‑marketplace — never suggests counterparties or unsolicited
    32→    actions
    33→
    34→-   Accessible — WCAG 2.2 Level AA compliant

   582→12G.7 Trade Health Score Engine
   583→
   584→12G.7.1 Purpose
   585→
   586→Provide a dynamic, at‑a‑glance health score for each trade (USTN) based
   587→on compliance, documentation, logistics, payment, risk, and timeline
   588→status.
   589→
   590→12G.7.2 Score Formula
   591→
   592→The Trade Health Score is a 0–100 composite calculated as:
   593→
   594→text
   595→
   596→Trade Health Score =
   597→
   598→(Compliance × 0.20) +
   599→
   600→(Documentation × 0.20) +
   601→
   602→(Logistics × 0.15) +
   603→
   604→(Payment × 0.15) +
   605→
   606→(Risk × 0.20) +
   607→
   608→(Timeline × 0.10)
   609→
   610→Where each component is a 0–100 sub‑score.
   611→
   612→12G.7.3 Component Definitions
   613→
   614→  ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
   615→  Component       What It Measures               Scoring Logic
   616→  --------------- ------------------------------ -----------------------------
   617→  Compliance      Sanctions clearance, KYB       100 = all cleared; 0 =
   618→                  status of parties,             sanctions hit or restricted
   619→                  jurisdiction risk              jurisdiction
   620→
   621→  Documentation   Percentage of required         (verified / required) × 100
   622→                  documents uploaded and         
   623→                  verified                       
   624→
   625→  Logistics       Status of logistics providers  100 = all mandatory providers
   626→                  (quoted, selected, signed      signed; 50 = some pending; 0
   627→                  addendum)                      = none
   628→
   629→  Payment         Fee payment status (SGTX fee,  100 = all paid; 50 = partial;
   630→                  Stage 1, deferred)             0 = none
   631→
   632→  Risk            Inverse of GNN risk score (if  (100 — risk_score)
   633→                  available) or platform risk    
   634→
   635→  Timeline        Milestone delays vs. expected  100 = on track; decreases by
   636→                  dates                          10 per day of delay (capped
   637→                                                 at 0)
